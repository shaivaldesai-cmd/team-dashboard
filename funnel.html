How these numbers are built
  1. Leads are new payroll deals created in the period, counted by the deal's create date. Leads the CRM routes to POS, Invoice, Tasks or PPU within five minutes of arriving are left out, as are deals deleted or merged away before the snapshot.
  2. Snapshot: each funnel is the CRM as it stood at 23:59 on the period's last day, rebuilt from the deal stage history. Last month's funnel is exactly what you'd have seen opening the CRM at that moment.
  3. Reached a stage means the deal got there at any point before the snapshot, even if it later moved on. A deal created straight into Demo Shown counts as arranged.
  4. Demo done means the deal reached Demo Shown, After Demo or Missed Sales, or closed after being arranged. This uses the stage history, not the demo-done-date field, because only the history shows what the CRM said on the day.
  5. Closed after demo counts deals that reached Deal Closed having been arranged. Deals closed without ever being arranged (direct payments) are listed separately.
  6. Where the rest sat is each lead's stage at the snapshot, for leads that hadn't reached demo arranged. These are not final: a lead in Call Not Connected at the snapshot may still convert later. Comparing both months at the same age is what makes the two funnels fair.
  7. Re-enquiries are older deals that came back into payroll Inbound during the period. They're counted but kept out of the funnel, since their earlier history would distort it.

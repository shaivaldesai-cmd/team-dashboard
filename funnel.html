How these numbers are built
  1. Leads are new payroll deals created in the period, by the deal's create date. Leads the CRM routes to POS, Invoice, Tasks or PPU within five minutes of arriving are left out, as are deals deleted or merged away before the snapshot. Re-enquiries on older deals are counted separately.
  2. Snapshot: both months are the CRM exactly as it stood at 23:59 on the period's last day, rebuilt from the deal stage history. The two periods cover the same days of the month.
  3. Connected means an answered call in either direction before the snapshot, or the deal reached demo arranged (so every arranged deal counts as connected, even if the call wasn't logged).
  4. Demo arranged / done / closed mean the deal reached that stage at any point before the snapshot, even if it later moved on. Later stages imply earlier ones. Demo done = Demo Shown, After Demo or Missed Sales, or closed after being arranged. Closed after demo excludes direct payments that were never arranged.
  5. Ended at the snapshot is where every lead sat at that moment; it sums to 100% of leads. "Still open" stages can still convert later.
  6. Calls come from the dialer's call stream: one row per call, answered = the dialer says answered or there was talk time, matched to the deal by phone number. Only calls made before the snapshot count.
  7. Speed to first call covers new payroll deals that arrived in Inbound between 10:30 and 19:00, excluding campaigns the dialer is told to skip, timed from the moment the deal was created in the CRM. The Overview page times new phone numbers from when our system first sees them, about 25 seconds later, so its figures run a few points higher; returning customers' new enquiries count here but not there.
